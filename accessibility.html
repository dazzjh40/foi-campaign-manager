Accessibility

We want this service to be usable by as many people as possible. We are working towards the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 AA standard.

This is a draft statement and will be completed before the product launches. It will cover how the service meets the standard, any known limitations, how to request content in an alternative format, and how to report an accessibility problem.

If you have an accessibility need or have found a barrier, please email darren.hale@144mboriginals.com.
